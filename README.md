## 📖 The Story Behind This Project

> *"What if the best team doesn't always win the trophy?"*

This question drove the entire analysis.

**Gujarat Titans** scored the most runs, took the most wickets, and played the most matches. By every statistical measure, they were the best team in IPL 2026. Yet on May 31, 2026, at the Narendra Modi Stadium in Ahmedabad, **Royal Challengers Bengaluru** lifted the trophy.

How? The data has the answer.

This project is a complete end-to-end analysis of **IPL 2026** — from raw ball-by-ball CSV data to a fully interactive Excel dashboard. Every chart, every insight, and every finding was built from scratch using Python.

## 💡 6 Key Insights from the Data

```
1. 🌟 SOORYAVANSHI EFFICIENCY
   Scored 776 runs in 342 balls
   Sai Sudharsan needed 479 balls for 722 runs
   That is 137 extra balls for 54 fewer runs
   The power of Strike Rate 226.90

2. 😲 THE BIGGEST UPSET
   GT dominated ALL stats all season
   Most runs | Most wickets | Most matches
   Yet RCB won the trophy on the final day

3. 🏟️ VENUE TELLS THE STORY
   Final was played at Ahmedabad — bowling ground
   RCB still chased 156 in just 18 overs
   Kohli SR in Final = 178 vs 162 all season

4. 💜 PURPLE CAP DRAMA
   Bhuvneshwar levelled with Rabada at 28 wickets
   Rabada took ONE wicket in the Final
   Won Purple Cap 29 to 28 — decided on last ball!

5. 📉 KKR BATTING COLLAPSE
   Scored only 2,311 runs in 14 matches
   772 fewer runs than GT in same matches
   Worst batting performance of the season

6. 🔴 KOHLI CLUTCH FACTOR
   Average SR all season = 162
   SR in the Final = 178
   Saved his absolute best for the biggest stage
```
